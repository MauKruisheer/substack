# Raw data

These two spreadsheets are copied, unchanged, from the Supplementary_Data folder of:

Cottom, J.W., Cook, E. & Velis, C.A. (2024). Data: A local-to-global emissions inventory of macroplastic pollution. Dryad. https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.8cz8w9gxb (licence: CC0 1.0)

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `SD_03_..._MFA_Outputs_National.xlsx` | model results per country (246 countries and territories, year 2020) |
| `SD_04_..._MFA_Outputs_Global_Regional_Income.xlsx` | results for the world, UN regions, OECD regions and World Bank income groups |

The full Dryad download (1.4 GB zip) also contains municipality-level results (SD_05, 660 MB) and the complete R model (SPOT). You don't need those for this project. Notebook `00_prepare_data` shows how to pull these two files out of the zip yourself.
